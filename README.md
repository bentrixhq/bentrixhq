**One application: laboratory, paper, real.**

- **Laboratory** — replay any market tick by tick, rewind a trade and take it again. A strategy reads the clock it is
  given and never the future.
- **Paper** — trade on live prices without sending an order anywhere.
- **Real** — trade your own account through your own terminal or exchange account. Bentrix never holds your broker's
  password.
- **Charts and rooms** — every chart type, indicators and drawings; private rooms where a team works on one chart.

**For quants and AI**

- **Strategies and validation** — a strategy is a versioned document, tested against chance before it is trusted.
- **Assay** — the scripting language that runs the scripts you already have.
- **SDK and agents** — everything a person can do in Bentrix, an agent can do through its MCP server and SDK, with your
  permission.

**What Bentrix is not.** It sells skill, craft and discipline — never profit. No signals, no broker links, no
introducing-broker rebates: [what we refuse](https://bentrix.net/en/refuse/).
